# Services and Fees

General consultation fees: RM25 for children under 12, RM35 for adults, and RM20 for senior citizens aged 60 and above. Medicine is charged separately.

Medical check-up package: RM150. It includes a blood test (full blood count, sugar, cholesterol), urine test and doctor review. Please fast for 8 hours before the blood test.

Vaccinations available: influenza (RM90) and hepatitis B (RM80 per dose). Please call the clinic for HPV vaccine prices.

Payment: cash, debit or credit card, DuitNow QR and selected panel insurance. Bring your IC and insurance card.
