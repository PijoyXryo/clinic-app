# Patient Policies

Medical certificates (MC) are only issued after a consultation with the doctor. We cannot issue backdated MCs.

Lab results are usually ready in 2 working days. You will receive a WhatsApp message when your results are ready.

Queue numbers are valid for the same day only. If you miss your number, please inform the front desk to be placed back in the queue.

Your medical records are confidential and handled according to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).
